How to play

  • Every answer comes straight from 1 & 2 Timothy or Titus (KJV).

  • Pick an answer, then stake how sure you are (1× Safe … 5× Preach It). A close distractor earns Grace — half the stake kept.

  • Wrong answers always show the verse and its reference.
  • Choose Your Hero: a 10-question run from Timothy or Titus — true/false, word order, and “who did this”.
  • Finish and get your Charge Report: grade, strengths, weaknesses, and how to do better.
